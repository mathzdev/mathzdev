## About

Engineering Lead in the Incubation area at Iron Mountain, working alongside distributed Product and Executive leadership in the US. Before Iron Mountain, I led architecture and delivery at Stefanini (financial systems processing 500M+ daily transactions with 99.8% uptime), Fluents.ai (multi-tenant AI voice-agent platform for the US market), BairesDev, and SNACKCLUB (one of Brazil's first PIX-to-crypto platforms).
